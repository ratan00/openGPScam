OpenGPS Cam is an open-source, ad-free camera that burns a GPS label into every photo: a minimap, the address, latitude and longitude, and the date, time and timezone.

• Live preview of the label over the viewfinder
• Never blocks the shutter; works offline with coordinates only and a simple map card
• Choose which fields to show, 12/24-hour time, decimal or DMS coordinates, strip position and opacity
• Optionally keep an unstamped original alongside the stamped photo
• No ads, no trackers, no Play Services

The minimap uses OpenStreetMap tiles (© OpenStreetMap contributors); turn it off to keep the app fully offline. Based on Fossify Camera.
